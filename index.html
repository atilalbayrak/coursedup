FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What exactly is CoursedUp?

Is CoursedUp an LMS?
No. An LMS primarily supports course delivery and administration. CoursedUp provides teaching visibility, allowing educators to build a public-facing teaching profile and a cross-institutional community focused on benchmarking.
Is it a course marketplace for students?
No. CoursedUp is centred on university educators and the practice behind their courses—not on selling courses to learners.
Who is it for?
University educators are the core users. Universities can also use Campus Plans to provide educator benefits and access institution-wide teaching analytics.
What makes it different from LinkedIn or ResearchGate?
Those platforms are broad professional or research networks. CoursedUp structures the experience specifically around university courses, weekly teaching activity, teaching methods and evidence of teaching practice.
Does the AI replace academic judgment?
No. It offers course-aware planning and activity suggestions. The educator remains responsible for selecting, adapting and applying what is appropriate.
Can a university use CoursedUp without replacing its existing systems?
Yes. CoursedUp Campus Plans are positioned as a teaching-visibility and analytics layer, not a replacement for the institution’s core learning-management system.
