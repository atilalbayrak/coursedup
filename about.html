What is CoursedUp?

Welcome to CoursedUp, a platform to showcase, report and enhance university teaching.

At CoursedUp, we have created a space where educators’ teaching can be visible to everyone outside the boundaries of their class and university. In addition to faculty visibility features, CoursedUp offers a place where educators interact to exchange ideas, generate their teaching analytics, and get AI-based assistance for advancement in university teaching.

With the motto of Let Your Course Live Forever! we aim to let educators promote their teaching and inspire each other.

What We Offer: Visibility

At CoursedUp, you can showcase your course progress outside the boundaries of your class and university. Also, you will be able to follow other courses around the World. Here’s what you can do:

Post. Take a few minutes each week to send out CoursedUp posts. Share detailed overviews of your sessions, what you taught, photos, videos from the class and the teaching strategies that worked. Select the teaching activity pursued from a rich set of teaching activities.

Get feedback for your posts. Get different means of feedback for your posts (Comment, Summary, Muddiest Point, Skills, and Like) from your students and peers.

Network. Join courses of others as peers or followers, and see their posts and activities on your feed.

Interact. Provide feedback to peers’ posts.

Discover.Discover how others in their discipline or university deliver their courses and find out new activities that they can apply in your courses for teaching, engagement, and assessment.

What We Offer: Assistance

CoursedUp features an AI-enhanced Assistant, AI-Agent Networks and Activity Library to help you improve your teaching. Here’s what you can do:

Get AI-based Assistance. Let CoursedUp suggest new activities to be used in the class. Learn key points to integrate them into your courses. CoursedUp’s AI assistant enables you to generate a course plan with suggested activities either for a specific week of the course or for the whole term.

Build a Network with AI-Agents. CoursedUp’s AI-generated Virtual Earth University, consists of hundreds of AI-generated agent professors from different areas that enables you to build a network and learn from them.

Explore innovative Teaching, Engagement, and Assessment (TEA) activities. CoursedUp offers more than 250 activities and their guidelines for your teaching advancement.

What We Offer: Analytics

CoursedUp offers unique teaching dashboards for both educators and institutions to generate teaching activity and interaction based analytics. Here’s what you can do:

Create course/educator analytics. With regular posts, create an evidence-based course t-analytics (activities and interactions), activity suggestions, and notifications from similar courses to enhance your teaching. Merge your course t-analytics into an educator t-analytics.

Benchmark. Discover how others in their discipline or university deliver their courses and find out new activities that they can apply in your courses for teaching, engagement, and assessment.

Institutional Analytics. Higher Education Institutions can also subscribe to our Campus Plans to observe Activity Analytics, Interaction Analytics, Comparative Analytics, External Units Comparative Analytics, Pairwise Comparison, Early Warning Analytics based on posts by their educators and to benchmark at different levels as university, school, department and program.

How CoursedUp is Different?

CoursedUp is the only platform designed to show the world the weekly progress of higher education courses. It is not:

  • A Learning Management System like Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard
  • A global online learning platform like Coursera, EdX, Udemy
  • A lecture delivery support tool like Mentimeter, Padlet, H5P, Vizia, Formative, Poll Everywhere
  • An instructor rating platform like Rate My Professor, Uloop, Koofers, Students Review
  • A course rating platform like Rate My Courses, Real Course Review, Class Ranked, Course Diggers
  • A study material sharing website like Studocu, Coursehero
  • A course communication/announcement platform for its students.

CoursedUp is uniquely positioned by its exclusive focus on Higher Education teaching, global scope, and integration of community, visibility, and assistance. Here are some key differences of CoursedUp from various other platfoms:

Platforms The CoursedUp Difference
General Professional Networkse.g. LinkedIn LinkedIn is a broad, general network. CoursedUp is hyper-focused on university teaching, structuring sharing specifically around courses and teaching activities rather than general career updates.
Learning Management Systemse.g. Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard LMSs are strictly internal operational tools. CoursedUp provides teaching visibility, allowing educators to build a public-facing teaching profile and a cross-institutional community focused on benchmarking.
Research Sharing Platformse.g. ResearchGate, Academia.edu These platforms are fundamentally research-focused. CoursedUp fills a crucial gap by providing a dedicated space to showcase, discuss, and improve actual teaching practices.
