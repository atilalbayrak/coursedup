From a course you teach
to evidence of how you teach.

One simple loop turns your weekly teaching into a public, ever-growing record — visible to peers, sharpened by AI, and backed by real analytics.

Share Your Progress

Sharing is optional — when you do want to share, it only takes a minute. Jot down a few quick notes and photos; AI turns them into a draft for you to review, tweak, and publish. Prefer to write it yourself? There’s plenty of room for the whole story.

04
Peer · Student · Follower

Grow a Social Teaching World

Peers, students and followers see your post and react with five feedback types — Like, Comment, Muddiest Point, Summary, Skill. Explore your discipline, department or country, and give feedback.

No extra work required. Every post, every activity and every piece of feedback quietly builds three kinds of analytics: Course and Educator t-Analytics for teaching, and Student l-Analytics for learning — benchmarked against your department, university and discipline, and exportable as evidence.

HOW COURSEDUP IS DIFFERENT?

CoursedUp is the only platform designed to show the world the weekly progress of higher education courses. It is not:

  • A Learning Management System like Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard
  • A global online learning platform like Coursera, EdX, Udemy
  • A lecture delivery support tool like Mentimeter, Padlet, H5P, Vizia, Formative, Poll Everywhere
  • An instructor rating platform like Rate My Professor, Uloop, Koofers, Students Review
  • A course rating platform like Rate My Courses, Real Course Review, Class Ranked, Course Diggers
  • A study material sharing website like Studocu, Coursehero
  • A course communication/announcement platform for its students.

CoursedUp is uniquely positioned by its exclusive focus on Higher Education teaching, global scope, and integration of community, visibility, and assistance. Here are some key differences of CoursedUp from various other platfoms:

Platforms The CoursedUp Difference
General Professional Networkse.g. LinkedIn LinkedIn is a broad, general network. CoursedUp is hyper-focused on university teaching, structuring sharing specifically around courses and teaching activities rather than general career updates.
Learning Management Systemse.g. Moodle, Canvas, Blackboard LMSs are strictly internal operational tools. CoursedUp provides teaching visibility, allowing educators to build a public-facing teaching profile and a cross-institutional community focused on benchmarking.
Research Sharing Platformse.g. ResearchGate, Academia.edu These platforms are fundamentally research-focused. CoursedUp fills a crucial gap by providing a dedicated space to showcase, discuss, and improve actual teaching practices.
