Good to know before you start

What counts as an active course?

Your course limit is the maximum number of courses you can have active in any given week.

Are weekly limits per course?

No. Post, AI-post, Assistant and VEU limits are weekly totals across all your courses.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel Premium or Premium+ in your account settings and keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do I have to provide a credit card info to start?

No. Starting CoursedUp's free plan requires no credit card information.

Quick answers for institutions

How are Campus Plans purchased?

They are arranged directly with your institution under a Campus Agreement, with invoicing or bank transfer.

How is pricing set?

By plan level, institutional scope and number of seats. More seats bring volume discounts.

Do we need IT support or an on-premise install?

No. CoursedUp uses only information educators choose to share publicly, so you can launch in a day.

Who decides which educators are included?

You do. Share the list of educators you want to upgrade and the plan level they should get.
