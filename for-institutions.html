THE INSTITUTIONAL DASHBOARD

Five analytics modules.Every level of your institution.

University→School→Department→Program→Course
01

Activity Analytics

A complete overview of active courses and teaching activities: total and unique activities, weekly frequency and weekly distribution trends. Activities are broken down by Teaching, Engagement and Assessment (TEA) and classified as basic, advanced or innovative.

Questions it answers
  • Are our instructors relying on basic methods, or integrating advanced and innovative activities?
  • What is the exact TEA breakdown in this program?
  • How many unique teaching activities are driving learning in this department this week?
02

Interaction Analytics

Network growth and engagement across your institution: approved peers, students and followers connected to your course networks, weekly interaction trends, and feedback received — comments, one-sentence summaries, muddiest points, skills and likes.

Questions it answers
  • Which courses are receiving the most “muddiest points” (areas of confusion) this week?
  • What is the average weekly interaction rate per course across the institution?
  • How fast is our academic network growing in peers, students and external followers this semester?
03

Comparative Analytics

Internal benchmarking of total activity, total interaction and peer, student and follower counts. The comparison scales with your view: schools at university level, departments at school level, programs at department level, and courses at program level.

Questions it answers
  • Which department in our faculty has the highest student and peer engagement this term?
  • Is our flagship program consistent — or are one or two courses doing all the heavy lifting?
  • How does total teaching activity in Engineering compare with Business?
04

External Units Comparative Analytics

Benchmark your unit against the wider world using key activity and interaction metrics. The baseline adapts automatically to the level you are viewing.

Questions it answers
  • How does our “activity per week per course” compare with national and global averages?
  • Is our Psychology department leading or lagging the national average for its discipline?
  • Is our flagship program outperforming the same programs at other universities in the country?
05

Early Warning Analytics

Spot problems while the semester can still be saved. By comparing the selected week with the previous one, the panel highlights Post Fill Rates, courses with no posts, and significant drops in activity or interaction.

Questions it answers
  • Which courses have gone completely silent this week?
  • Has interaction across the university dropped sharply since last week?
  • Which programs show a declining Post Fill Rate that may signal instructor burnout or student disengagement?
  • Which departments are at highest risk of student drop-off right now?
