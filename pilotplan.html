What you get during the pilot

For your educators

  • Practical resources for faculty development. Give participating instructors opportunities to discover teaching activities, develop new ideas with AI, and explore approaches beyond their usual classroom routines.
  • Opportunities for collegial exchange. Explore ways to make teaching practices more discoverable and encourage voluntary sharing among colleagues and departments.
  • An opportunity to shape the platform. Share practical feedback that helps inform CoursedUp's development for real university teaching environments.

For your institution

  • Evidence to guide future decisions. Evaluate real usage, repeat engagement, instructor feedback, and implementation barriers before considering a broader initiative.
  • A direct founding-partner relationship. Work directly with the CoursedUp team during the pilot and contribute to shaping its future direction.
  • Complimentary pilot participation. Selected institutions receive the agreed pilot access and support without a participation fee for the defined pilot period. There is no obligation to purchase a subscription or expand after the pilot.
